The Art of the Cut

At Obsidian, we believe that exceptional dining is theater. Every detail, from the dimly lit atmosphere to the precise orchestration of service, is designed to elevate the pure, unadulterated flavor of the world's finest cuts.

Sourced from exclusive ranches and aged to perfection in our custom Himalayan salt chambers, our steaks are an expression of dedication to the craft. Step into the shadows and let the spotlight fall on the flame.
